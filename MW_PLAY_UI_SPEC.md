# MW Play UI spec

## Navigation

Desktop/TV target order: **Início · Filmes · Séries · Em alta · Minha lista · Pesquisar**, with Configurações and Minha conta visually separated near the bottom. Mobile keeps high-frequency actions in the bottom navigation.

## Home

The target home experience is streaming-first: a large featured hero followed by Continue assistindo, Em alta, Filmes and Séries rails. Artwork should dominate over controls.

## Cards

Use softer 0.7–0.9rem corners, restrained metadata, visible progress bars for Continue assistindo, and a purple/blue focus treatment suitable for mouse, keyboard and TV remotes.

## Details

Backdrop-led layout with title, year, genre, duration/season information and a strong primary **Assistir agora** action. Episode navigation should be simpler than the upstream information density.

## Player

Preserve the mature playback behavior. Restyle controls without removing audio-track selection, subtitles, subtitle timing, casting or external-player support.

## Responsive targets

- Phone: one-hand navigation and bottom actions
- Desktop: artwork-rich wide layout
- TV: 10-foot readability, large focus targets and predictable spatial navigation

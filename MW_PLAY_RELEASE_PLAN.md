# MW Play release plan

1. **Foundation preview** — branding/theme only; verify build and upstream functionality.
2. **Home preview** — new navigation, hero and streaming rails.
3. **Playback preview** — details/player styling and TV focus polish.
4. **Beta** — replace final icons/splash assets, test supported devices, accessibility and account isolation.
5. **Release** — distribute only with authorized content/services and publish corresponding source/license materials as required by GPL-2.0.

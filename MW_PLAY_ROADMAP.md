# MW Play UI Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Brand foundation
- [x] MW Play application name and metadata
- [x] Purple/blue cinematic palette
- [x] MW logo asset
- [x] Independent redesign branch
- [ ] Replace remaining upstream visual branding assets
- [ ] MW Play PWA icons and splash assets

## Phase 2 — Streaming-first home
- Hero area with featured content
- Continue Watching as a high-priority row
- Dedicated Movies, Series and Trending discovery sections
- Larger artwork and reduced interface clutter
- My List shortcut

## Phase 3 — Details and player
- Cinematic metadata page
- Cleaner season/episode browser
- Simplified source selection presentation
- Preserve audio tracks, subtitles, subtitle delay, casting and external-player controls

## Phase 4 — TV experience
- Strong remote-focus states
- Larger hit targets and typography
- Side navigation optimized for 10-foot viewing
- Test spatial navigation on Android/Google TV

## Product principle
MW Play should remain source-agnostic. The interface should not depend on any particular third-party addon being available. Content offered commercially should be appropriately licensed or authorized.

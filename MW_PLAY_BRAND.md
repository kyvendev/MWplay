# MW Play brand system

## Core palette

- Cinema black: `#080913`
- Deep navy: `#11132A`
- MW purple: `#8B35FF`
- Electric blue: `#3F9CFF`
- Cyan highlight: `#36C9FF`
- Primary text: `#FFFFFF`
- Secondary text: `#B9BDD2`

## UI character

MW Play should feel cinematic rather than dashboard-like: dark uninterrupted surfaces, generous artwork, low-contrast chrome, rounded cards, and bright gradient focus/primary actions.

## Logo assets

- `images/mw-play-logo.svg` — expressive MW + play symbol
- `images/mw-play-mark.svg` — square app mark
- `images/mw-play-wordmark.svg` — MW PLAY wordmark/tagline lockup

These are original MW Play assets and are intentionally separate from upstream Stremio artwork.
